# UKR01 v2 — stair landing correction

## Finding

V1 had two successive straight flights along the first bay (y=0→2.4 and 3.6→6). Its intermediate landing was present, but the next storey's first flight began at y=0 on the external building edge, without a floor landing in front of it. The available slab alongside the narrow opening did not supply a proper bottom landing. Merely sharing the front beam node did not make this a usable circulation route.

## Corrected nominal arrangement

The first 4 × 6 m bay now contains a return stair:

- Lower flight: x=1 m, y=1.5→3.9 m, rises floor→floor+1.5 m.
- Upper flight: x=3 m, y=3.9→1.5 m, rises floor+1.5→next floor.
- Floor landing: x=0→4 m, y=0→1.5 m, at every floor including ground and roof. Arriving and departing flights share this platform, which adjoins the rest of the floor.
- Intermediate landing: x=0→4 m, y=3.9→5.4 m, at each half-storey. Two adjoining 2 m wide strips cover the complete turning platform.
- Each flight remains 1.2 m wide, with 1.5 m rise, 2.4 m horizontal run and nine illustrative risers. The two flight envelopes do not overlap in plan.
- Nominal landing depth is 1.5 m, rather than the 1.2 m flight width. An edge beam's 0.15 m half-width leaves 1.35 m of nominal plan depth. This is a plan-space check only, not an as-built clearance certificate.
- The elevated floor opening is now 4 × 3.9 m. The ground bay is filled, with nodes at the actual flight/landing connections.

[Approved Document K for England](https://assets.publishing.service.gov.uk/government/uploads/system/uploads/attachment_data/file/996860/Approved_Document_K.pdf), paragraph 1.20, describes top/bottom landing dimensions relative to flight width. This is a relevant basic layout reference, not proof of compliance for a British project whose nation, use category and detailed design remain unconfirmed.

## Actual model/load changes

Floor and intermediate landings are persisted physical objects, each owning the real analysis strips. Each flight end shares an actual node ID with a landing. Intermediate strips terminate on a beam at y=5.4 m; that beam connects through the side beams to columns. The reference idealises these strips as monolithic cantilevers from the support beams, not unsupported decorative platforms.

Revised floor/roof area is 657.6 m². Floor landings total 30 m² and are already included in that area; their 3 kPa imposed load replaces the respective floor/roof load. Intermediate landings add 24 m² with their own self weight, finishes and imposed load. Stair flight weights/projected loads remain unchanged. The independent quantity take-off was amended for this intentional geometry change; numerical tolerances and existing analytical fixtures were not changed.

V2 contains 447 nodes, 642 analytical members and 212 physical objects. It is generated by `residential_reference()` as UKR01-v2. ## Limits of this check

The reference remains a gross, uncracked, centreline frame/strip idealisation. Its generic beam/slab envelopes do not establish finished floor alignment or physical eccentric offsets. A beam drawn through the floor datum must not be interpreted as an approved upstand across a circulation route. Treads are illustrative; exact nosing/waist/landing junctions, clear headroom to beams and slabs, guard/handrail space, doors, escape/accessibility and reinforcement/detailing remain unverified. Those limitations require a physical-level/offset and architectural detailing model; no geometry, code or safety PASS is issued for them.
